Zabiha Halal Mediterranean Food in Rogers Park: Your Guide to Milo's Menu

Rotisserie shawarma, flame-grilled kabobs, crispy falafel and dips made fresh every day, all on Morse Ave, a short walk from the Red Line.

If you live, work or study in Rogers Park and you're searching for halal food near you, you don't have to go far. Milo's Pita at 1335 W Morse Ave serves Mediterranean classics made with 100% zabiha halal meat, plus a long list of vegetarian and vegan dishes. Here's what's on the menu and how to make the most of it.

What "zabiha halal" means at Milo's

Zabiha refers to the traditional Islamic method of slaughter, the standard many Muslim diners look for when eating out. At Milo's, every meat on the menu (beef shawarma, chicken shawarma, steak, chicken and kefta kabobs) is zabiha halal. So whether you order a quick wrap or a full combo plate, you never have to ask twice.
